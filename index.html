About the Game

It's a chill mystery game where you explore royal rooms and solve
puzzles.
Queen’s Hidden Match offers a calming gameplay experience where players explore beautifully designed royal environments while solving match-based challenges and searching for hidden objects. Each level introduces new palace locations, decorative elements, and increasingly creative puzzle mechanics. The whole point is to make it look gorgeous and feel relaxing - no stress, just beautiful rooms and a good story. The overall mood should feel luxurious, cozy, mysterious, and visually polished.
